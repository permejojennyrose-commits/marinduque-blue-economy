KNOW BEFORE YOU GO

Check local fishing zones first.

Marine protected area limits, seasonal closures, and allowed gear vary by location. Check with your municipal agriculture office or local FARMC before heading out—this guide is not a boundary map or a live advisory.

01 / ZONES◉

Give habitats room

Respect locally posted no-take areas and closures. Keep fishing gear away from coral reefs, seagrass beds, mangroves, and marked restoration sites.

Ask your local FARMC
02 / GEAR⌁

Choose selective gear

Use only gear permitted for your area. Avoid destructive methods and abandoned gear; retrieve lost lines and nets when it is safe to do so.

Follow municipal rules
03 / SAFETY↗

Bring the basics

Wear a life jacket, check your boat and fuel, bring communication and first-aid supplies, and let someone know your route and return time.

Safety over the catch

General educational guidance only—not legal advice, a navigation chart, or real-time weather, sea-condition, or hazard data. Follow current directions from your LGU and relevant authorities.
